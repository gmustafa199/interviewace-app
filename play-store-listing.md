## Full Description (4000 chars max)

InterviewAce is your personal AI interviewer — practice realistic mock interviews for the most in-demand IT roles, get instant honest feedback, and walk into your real interview with confidence.

🎯 **8 SPECIALIZED IT ROLES**
- Software Engineer (SWE)
- Frontend Engineer
- Backend Engineer
- Data Scientist
- DevOps Engineer
- ML Engineer
- Cloud Engineer
- Product Manager

Each role has role-specific questions, technical depth calibration, and a realistic interview flow that mirrors what you'll actually face at companies like Google, Amazon, Meta, Stripe, and top startups.

🎤 **VOICE MODE — MOST REALISTIC**
Speak your answers out loud. The AI interviewer talks back with natural-sounding questions, just like a real interview. Train your speaking confidence, not just your typing. Pro feature.

📊 **HONEST SCORECARDS, NOT HYPE**
After every interview, get a detailed scorecard with:
- Overall score (0-10)
- Per-dimension breakdown (technical depth, communication, problem-solving, code quality where applicable)
- Sample better answers for each question
- A 7-day personalized practice plan based on your weak spots

No "great job!" platitudes. Just real, actionable feedback so you actually improve.

⚡ **CHOOSE YOUR DIFFICULTY**
- Beginner — warm-up questions, gentle pace
- Intermediate — standard interview rigor
- Senior — system design, deep follow-ups, edge cases

Choose 5, 8, or 12 questions per session depending on how much time you have.

🔥 **WHY INTERVIEWACE BEATS WATCHING VIDEOS**
- Active practice > passive watching. You retain 10× more by doing.
- Instant feedback — no waiting for a human mentor.
- Private — practice your awkward answers in your bedroom, not a Zoom call with a stranger.
- Repeatable — do 10 interviews a day if you want. Cost is the same.

💳 **PRICING**
- Free: 3 mock interviews per month, text mode, all 8 roles
- Pro ($19/month): Unlimited interviews, voice mode, 7-day practice plans, scorecard history

Subscriptions auto-renew monthly. Cancel anytime from Google Play.

🔒 **PRIVACY**
Your interview transcripts and scorecards are private to your account. Voice recordings are processed in real-time and not stored. We never sell your data.

Built by developers who've been on both sides of the interview table.
